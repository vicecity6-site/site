GTA6 Clips Uploader

Operated by Daniel · Contact: cbmyahta@gmail.com

GTA6 Clips Uploader is a private, internal tool. It prepares short vertical gaming clips and uploads them to YouTube channels owned and operated by the same person who runs the tool (ViceCity6 ClipChaos and its language versions).

How it uses YouTube

The tool signs in with Google only to the owner's own YouTube channels and uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time. It does not read, collect or analyse data about other YouTube users.

Content and permissions

Clips are made from gameplay videos and streams with permission from the original creators. Every video description credits the original creator and links to their channel.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There is no public sign-up and no third-party users.
